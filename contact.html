Frequently Asked Questions

What types of clothing do you accept?

We accept clean, gently used clothing for all ages, shoes, accessories, and special occasion wear. Items should be in good condition without stains or damage.

Do you offer pickup services?

Yes! We offer free pickup services for large donations. Simply fill out our pickup request form on the donate page, and we'll contact you to schedule.

Can I get a tax receipt for my donations?

Absolutely! We provide tax receipts for all monetary donations and can provide documentation for clothing donations. We're a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

How can I volunteer?

We have many volunteer opportunities! Contact us using the form above with "Volunteer Opportunities" as the subject, and we'll get back to you with current openings.

Do you have a wishlist of needed items?

Yes! We particularly need professional clothing, winter coats, children's clothing, and shoes. Check our social media for current specific needs.

How do you help people in the community?

We partner with local shelters, job centers, and schools to identify families in need. We also have a dignified shopping experience where people can choose items that fit their needs.
