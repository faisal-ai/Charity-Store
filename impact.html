Environmental Impact

How we're helping the planet

Sustainable Fashion Matters

The fashion industry is one of the world's largest polluters. By giving clothing a second life, we're making a real environmental impact:

  • 15 tons of textile waste diverted from landfills
  • 2.3 million gallons of water saved (equivalent to what's used to make new clothes)
  • 45 tons of CO2 emissions prevented
  • Circular economy promoted through reuse and recycling

🌍 Every Item Counts

When you donate or purchase from us, you're part of a sustainable solution that benefits both people and the planet.
