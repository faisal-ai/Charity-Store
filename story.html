Our Story

Learn about our mission to provide clothing while protecting the environment

About Charity Store

Charity Store was founded in 2019 with a simple mission: to provide quality clothing to those in need while promoting sustainable fashion practices. What started as a small community initiative has grown into a thriving charity that has helped thousands of families.

Our Mission

To create a world where everyone has access to quality clothing, regardless of their economic situation, while promoting environmental sustainability through clothing reuse and recycling.

Our Vision

We envision communities where clothing insecurity is eliminated, and sustainable fashion practices are the norm.

Our Journey

Key milestones in our story

2019

The Beginning

Charity Store was founded by Sarah Johnson with a simple goal: help families in need access quality clothing.

2020

First Milestone

We helped our first 500 families and established partnerships with local shelters and community centers.

2021

Growth & Expansion

Opened our first physical location and launched our online store, making clothing accessible to even more people.

2022

Community Impact

Reached 2,000 families helped and launched our environmental sustainability program.

2023

Recognition

Received the Community Service Award and expanded our volunteer program.

2024

Looking Forward

Over 3,000 families helped and continuing to grow our impact in the community.
