ABOUT BU MENTORING CIC

BU Mentoring CIC is a community interest company based in Brompton, Medway, Kent. Since 2013, we have supported young people, adults, and vulnerable individuals in rebuilding their confidence, gaining practical skills, and moving into employment, apprenticeships, or self-employment.

We primarily work with young people aged 16 to 26, including those who are NEET, from disadvantaged backgrounds, or facing social isolation. We also support adults experiencing long term unemployment and vulnerability.

We believe that within every individual lies untapped potential. Many young people do not lack ability. They lack structured guidance, belief and opportunity.

Our mentoring model focuses first on confidence and identity development. We then build employability skills, enterprise readiness and academic foundations through tailored one to one mentoring, accredited tuition, work experience and practical career support.

Alongside skills development, we remove immediate barriers to progress by providing food parcels, clothing support and social care assistance within our community hub.

We exist to turn vulnerability into capability and isolation into contribution.

Our Mission

Our mission is to equip young people and vulnerable adults with the confidence, skills and structured mentoring required to access employment, apprenticeships or self employment.

We achieve this through:
• One to one mentoring
• Career guidance and CV preparation
• Work experience and employer engagement
• Self employment assessment and enterprise support
• GCSE tuition in Maths and English
• Community based practical support that removes barriers to progress.

Our Vision

To build a generation of resilient, confident and economically independent young people who actively contribute to their communities and break cycles of dependency.

Our Journey

Key milestones in our story

2019

The Beginning

Charity Store was founded by Sarah Johnson with a simple goal: help families in need access quality clothing.

2020

First Milestone

We helped our first 500 families and established partnerships with local shelters and community centers.

2021

Growth & Expansion

Opened our first physical location and launched our online store, making clothing accessible to even more people.

2022

Community Impact

Reached 2,000 families helped and launched our environmental sustainability program.

2023

Recognition

Received the Community Service Award and expanded our volunteer program.

2024

Looking Forward

Over 3,000 families helped and continuing to grow our impact in the community.
