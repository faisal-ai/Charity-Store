👕 Clothing Donation

Donate gently used clothing and accessories

We Accept:

  • ✅ Clean, gently used clothing
  • ✅ Shoes in good condition
  • ✅ Accessories (bags, belts, jewelry)
  • ✅ Winter coats and jackets
  • ✅ Professional wear
  • ✅ Children's clothing
  • ✅ Sports and activewear
  • ✅ Formal and special occasion wear

Please Don't Bring:

  • ❌ Heavily worn or damaged items
  • ❌ Stained or dirty clothing
  • ❌ Underwear or swimwear
  • ❌ Items with strong odors
